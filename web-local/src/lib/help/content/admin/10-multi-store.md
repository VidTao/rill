# Multi-store accounts

Run more than one brand? Bratrax can keep all of them under a single login as a
**multi-store account** — each store with its own dashboards, connections, and
data, and a quick way to move between them without signing in and out.

## Add a store
Use the **Add store** button in the top navigation bar. Give the new store a
name; Bratrax sets it up and walks you through connecting it — store, tracker, ad
platforms — from [Connectors](https://bratrax.com/connectors), exactly like your
first one.

You can do this yourself any time. Adding your **second** store turns your
account into a multi-store account automatically — there's no separate upgrade
step.

**No extra charge.** New stores are covered by your existing subscription, so
they skip the payment step and start connecting right away.

## Switch between stores
Once you have more than one store, a **store picker** appears in the navigation
bar. Pick a store and the whole app — dashboards,
[Connectors](https://bratrax.com/connectors),
[Settings](https://bratrax.com/settings/account) — switches to that store. Each
store's data stays completely separate.

## Who can do what
- **Admins** can add stores and switch between them.
- **View-only teammates** see the stores they've been given access to, but can't
  add new ones.

## Billing
One subscription covers the whole account. Your plan and payment live on the
parent account — manage them from
[Settings → Billing](https://bratrax.com/settings/billing) on any store. There's
no extra charge to add a store.
