# Billing & subscription

## How do I manage my subscription, payment methods, or invoices?

Everything subscription-related lives in our billing portal, hosted by our payment provider.

**To get there:** Open [bratrax.com/settings](https://bratrax.com/settings), go to the [**Billing**](https://bratrax.com/settings/billing) tab, and click **Manage Subscription**. That takes you to the portal at [join.bratrax.com/billing](https://join.bratrax.com/billing).

**How sign-in works:** The portal asks for the email address you used to purchase your subscription. Enter it, and our payment provider emails you a sign-in link — click it and you're in. No password to remember.

**What you can do inside the portal:**

- Update or replace your payment method
- View your full invoice history and download any invoice as a PDF
- Update billing details (company name, tax ID, address)
- Pause or cancel your subscription

If your email link doesn't arrive within a minute or two, check spam, and confirm you used the exact email that matches your Bratrax account. If you're still stuck, email [support@bratrax.com](mailto:support@bratrax.com).

## If you installed from the Shopify App Store

Your subscription is billed by **Shopify**, not through our billing portal — so the steps above (join.bratrax.com/billing) don't apply to you.

- **Change your plan:** Open [**Settings → Billing**](https://bratrax.com/settings/billing) in Bratrax and click **Manage Subscription** — it takes you to Shopify's hosted plan page, where you can upgrade or downgrade.
- **Payment method & invoices:** these live in your **Shopify admin** (Settings → Billing), alongside your other Shopify charges.
- **Cancel:** uninstall the Bratrax app from your Shopify admin — Shopify ends the subscription when the app is removed.
