# Onboarding & getting unstuck

## How do I resume onboarding if I got interrupted?

In most cases, you don't need to do anything special — the next time you log in, Bratrax will drop you straight onto the screen that needs your attention. The product remembers where you left off.

If you skipped a step or want to double-check the state of your setup manually:

- **Review which integrations are connected and which are still pending:** [bratrax.com/connectors](https://bratrax.com/connectors)
- **Account-level settings (API keys, profile, billing):** [bratrax.com/settings](https://bratrax.com/settings)

Once your last integration finishes connecting, dashboards populate within about 10 minutes. If you're stuck on a specific step that won't move forward, email [support@bratrax.com](mailto:support@bratrax.com) — a real person will get back to you, usually within a few hours on business days.
