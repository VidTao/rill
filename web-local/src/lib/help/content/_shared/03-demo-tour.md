# Touring the Bratrax demo

You're in the **Bratrax Demo Account** — a full, working Bratrax workspace
running on a synthetic store. Every dashboard, metric, and drilldown behaves
exactly as it does for a real customer. The only difference is the data: the
orders, ad spend, and customers are generated, not a real brand's.

You have **viewer** access, so you can explore everything and change nothing.
Click around freely — you can't break it.

This tour takes about ten minutes. Do it in order, or jump to whatever you came
to see.

---

## 1. Store Performance — the daily health check

The one dashboard you'd open every morning. Revenue, returns, ad spend, MER,
blended ROAS, new-vs-returning mix, subscription health — the whole business on
one screen.

- **Try:** find yesterday's total sales, then switch the time range to compare
  it against the previous period.
- **Go deeper:** [Store Performance](/help/viewer/store-performance)

## 2. Attribution — where the sales actually came from

This is the point of Bratrax. The Attribution dashboard drills from channel →
source → campaign → ad set → ad, with the media-buyer metrics on top: MER,
new-customer ROAS and CPA, contribution margin.

The part worth your attention is the **blue numbers**. They're clickable. Click
a sales figure and you get the actual orders behind it; click an order and you
get that customer's real path from first touch to purchase — every ad, email,
and visit, in order. That evidence trail is what separates a number you can act
on from a number a pixel guessed at.

- **Try:** expand the biggest channel down to the ad level, click one blue sales
  number, then open a single customer journey and read it end to end.
- **Go deeper:** [Attribution](/help/viewer/attribution) ·
  [Filters and time range](/help/viewer/filters-and-time)

## 3. Products — what's selling

Product, variant, and SKU-level truth: net sales, units, discounts, top
performers.

- **Try:** find the top SKU by net sales this month.
- **Go deeper:** [Products](/help/viewer/products)

## 4. Customer Analytics — who's buying, and whether they stay

Customer base, LTV, cohorts, retention, MRR, and which campaigns produce
subscribers rather than one-time buyers.

- **Try:** find the average customer LTV, then the single biggest
  subscriber-acquisition source.
- **Go deeper:** [Customer Analytics](/help/viewer/customer-analytics)

## 5. Email & SMS — the Klaviyo side

Campaigns and flows: sends, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, list health, and the
revenue the email program actually drove.

- **Try:** check the open rate on the latest campaign, then glance at list
  health for rising bounces.
- **Go deeper:** [Email & SMS](/help/viewer/email-sms)

## 6. Profile Explorer — the people who haven't bought yet

Most analytics tools only show you buyers. Bratrax builds a profile for
everyone it can see — customers, known leads, and anonymous visitors — segments
them (abandoned checkout, high-intent anonymous, winback) and suggests what to
do with each group.

- **Try:** in Profile Explorer, count the reachable non-buyers. In Commerce
  Profile Graph, find which acquisition channel produces the highest-LTV
  customers.
- **Go deeper:** [Profile Explorer](/help/viewer/profile-explorer) ·
  [Commerce Profile Graph](/help/viewer/commerce-profile-graph)

## What the demo can't show you

Your own numbers. On a real workspace you'd connect Shopify or WooCommerce plus
your ad platforms, and Bratrax would backfill your history and start matching
orders to touchpoints within the hour. The dashboards you just walked through
are the ones you'd get, filled with your data.

The full product tour — including the admin side (connecting platforms, cost
settings, metric trees) — is at
[Start here: your first hour with Bratrax](/help/start-here).
