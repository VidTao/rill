# Touring the Bratrax demo

You're in the **Bratrax Demo Account** — a full, working Bratrax workspace
running on a synthetic store. Every dashboard, metric, and drilldown behaves
exactly as it does for a real customer. The only difference is the data: the
orders, ad spend, and customers are generated, not a real brand's.

You have **viewer** access, so you can explore everything and change nothing.
Click around freely — you can't break it.

Seven stops, about fifteen minutes — or half an hour if you watch every
walkthrough. Each one has a short video, something to **try** yourself, and
somewhere to **go deeper**. Do them in order, or jump to whatever you came to
see.

---

## 1. Find your way around

Every dashboard has the same controls at the top, and they're where most of the
value is — the default view is just a starting point.

- **Time range** — Last 7 days for a check-in, Last 30 for campaign and product
  review, Last 90 for customer and subscription patterns.
- **Comparison** — previous period or previous year, so every number arrives
  with context instead of sitting there alone.
- **Filters** — click **+ Filter** to narrow by channel, campaign, country,
  product. Multiple filters combine with AND.
- **Click-to-filter** — most charts and table rows filter the whole dashboard
  when you click them. It's the fastest way to chase something down.

- **Try:** set the range to **Last 30 days** with **previous period**
  comparison, then add one filter and remove it again with the × on its chip.
- **Go deeper:** [Filters and time ranges](/help/viewer/filters-and-time) ·
  [Reading the dashboards](/help/viewer/reading-dashboards)

## 3. Attribution — where the sales actually came from

Spend most of your time here.
[**Attribution**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/campaign_deep_dive) drills from
channel → campaign → ad set → ad, with the media-buyer metrics on top: MER,
new-customer ROAS and CPA, contribution margin. It defaults to **last-touch** —
the last eligible source before the purchase gets credit.

The part worth your attention is the **blue numbers**. They're clickable. Click
a sales figure and you get the actual orders behind it; click an order and you
get that customer's real path from first touch to purchase — every ad, email,
and visit, in order. That evidence trail is what separates a number you can act
on from a number a pixel guessed at.

```loom
id: c3a62d5c20d245aab5f86f578a5a851e
label: Attribution
duration: 1:34
```

- **Try:** sort the table by spend to find the biggest channel, click it to
  filter the dashboard, then expand down to the ad level. Now click a blue
  **Attributed Orders** number, pick one order, and read the journey: every
  touchpoint, the evidence behind each match, and which touch won the credit.
- **Go deeper:** [Attribution](/help/viewer/attribution) ·
  [Filters and time ranges](/help/viewer/filters-and-time)

## 7. Profile Explorer — the people who haven't bought yet

Most tools stop at people who bought.
[**Profile Explorer**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/profile_explorer) covers
everyone Bratrax can see — customers, known leads, and anonymous visitors —
grouped into segments, each with a recommended action.
[**Commerce Profile Graph**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/commerce_profile_graph)
is the same picture in aggregate: how much of the audience is reachable, and
where the best customers come from.

your ad platforms, and Bratrax would backfill your history and start matching
orders to touchpoints within the hour. The dashboards you just walked through are
the ones you'd get, filled with your data. **$1 for your first month, then $99/mo
flat at any GMV** — run it beside whatever you use today and compare the two
before you decide anything.
