# Asking Claude

Claude is a built-in analyst. Open the chat panel and ask questions about your data in plain English — it answers using the same metrics and dimensions that power your dashboards.

## How to phrase a good question

The clearer your question, the better the answer. Include three things:

- **The metric** — ROAS, revenue, orders, LTV, conversion rate
- **The time window** — last week, October, year to date, since the last campaign launched
- **What to compare or break down by** — by channel, by product, vs. last month

A vague question like *"how am I doing?"* gets a vague answer. *"How did ROAS perform last week vs. the week before, broken down by channel?"* gets a useful one.

## Example questions

**Performance**
- "How did revenue compare to last month?"
- "Which day this week had the best ROAS?"

**Diagnostics**
- "Why did Facebook spend jump on Tuesday?"
- "What's driving the drop in conversion rate this week?"

**Channel and campaign**
- "Top 5 campaigns by ROAS in the last 30 days"
- "Which channels have the highest LTV customers?"

**Product and customer**
- "Top SKUs by AOV last quarter"
- "How are customers acquired in October performing this month?"

## What Claude can do

- Read every metric, dimension, and dashboard in your account
- Pull live numbers as of the last data refresh
- Compare periods, calculate ratios, group results
- Explain what's driving a change

## What Claude can't do

- See data younger than the last refresh (typically 1–3 hours old)
- Modify your dashboards or data
- Pull from sources you haven't connected
- Take actions outside the chat

## When the answer looks wrong

If a number from Claude doesn't match what you see on a dashboard, tell it: *"That doesn't match the dashboard for the same period — can you check?"* Usually it's a phrasing issue and a clarification fixes it. If it persists, contact support with the question and the answer you got.
