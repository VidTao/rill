# Welcome to Bratrax

Bratrax is where you answer the operating questions behind your store: are sales healthy, which channels are working, which products are carrying the business, and whether customers are coming back.

Start with the dashboards. Each one is built for a different job.

## Start here

| If you want to know... | Open this dashboard |
|---|---|
| Is the business healthy today? | [Store Performance](https://bratrax.com/canvas/performance_overview) |
| Where did sales come from? | [Attribution](https://bratrax.com/canvas/campaign_deep_dive) |
| Which products and SKUs are selling? | [Products](https://bratrax.com/canvas/product_performance) |

## Your first 5 checks

1. Open [**Store Performance**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/performance_overview) and check Total Sales, MER, Paid ROAS, spend, returns, and new-customer orders.
2. Open [**Attribution**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/campaign_deep_dive) and look at channel, campaign, ad set, and ad performance on last-touch attribution.
3. Open [**Products**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/product_performance) and confirm which products and SKUs are driving sales.
4. Open [**Customer Analytics**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/customer_analytics) and check MRR, active subscribers, LTV, and subscriber acquisition.
5. Open [**Email & SMS**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/email_marketing) and check campaign/flow performance, unsubs, and attributed email sales.

## What changed?

Every dashboard starts with a date range and comparison. Most users should begin with **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days** and compare to the previous period.

If a KPI moved, do not stop at the tile. Scroll down, click into the channel, product, campaign, or customer group that moved, and use the charts and tables to find the driver.

## How fresh is the data

Dashboards refresh on a schedule — store orders hourly, all other integrations every 4 hours — so numbers are not live to the second. When you see "today," read it as "today as of the last successful refresh."

Use the dashboard's "as of" timestamp when a number looks stale or when you are comparing against Shopify or an ad platform.

## Good first Claude questions

- "What changed in Store Performance over the last 7 days?"
- "Which channels drove the change in Attributed Sales?"
- "Which products contributed most to the sales change?"
- "Which campaigns acquired new customers at the lowest CPA?"
- "Why is Direct high in the selected period?"
