# Email & SMS

Use [**Email & SMS**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/email_marketing) to understand lifecycle marketing: campaigns, flows, sends, opens, clicks, unsubs, and email-attributed sales.

## Look first at

- **Campaigns & Flows** — performance by one-off campaign and automated flow.
- **Attributed Sales** — sales attributed to email/SMS through Bratrax attribution.
- **Open Rate / Click Rate** — engagement quality.
- **Unsubscribes** — audience fatigue or deliverability warning.
- **Lifetime Program Context** — long-term email/SMS contribution.

## How to read it

Start with the **Campaigns & Flows** table. It groups one-off campaigns and automated flows so you can compare them separately.

Then check **List Health**:

- Sends show volume.
- Opens show attention.
- Bounces show deliverability pressure.
- Unsubscribes show fatigue or poor targeting.

Use the lifetime section for context, not daily operations. It is not affected by the selected date range.

## Common misreads

- **Daily rates can be misleading.** A send on one day can receive opens and clicks on later days.
- **Klaviyo revenue and Bratrax attributed sales may differ.** Bratrax uses its attribution model; Klaviyo may use its own.
- **Flows and campaigns should not be judged the same way.** Flows are always-on; campaigns are one-off sends.
- **Unsubs need volume context.** A spike after a large send may be normal; a spike on normal volume is more concerning.

## Drilldown moves

- If attributed sales dropped, check whether sends dropped or click rate dropped.
- If opens fell while sends stayed flat, inspect subject lines and deliverability.
- If clicks fell after opens stayed stable, inspect offer and content.
- If unsubs rose, inspect which campaign or flow caused it.

## Good Claude questions

- "Which email campaigns drove the most attributed sales last 30 days?"
- "Did open rate or click rate cause the email sales change?"
- "Which flows have high unsubscribes?"
- "Compare campaigns and flows by attributed sales and engagement."
