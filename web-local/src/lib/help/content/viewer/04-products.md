# Products

Use [**Products**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/product_performance) to understand what actually sold: products, variants, SKUs, units, discounts, and net sales.

## Look first at

- **Product Net Sales** — product-level sales after line-item discounts.
- **Units Sold** — quantity sold, independent of price.
- **Discounts** — how much product revenue was reduced.
- **Top Products** — which products contributed most to sales.
- **Top SKUs** — which variants or SKUs are carrying demand.

## How to read it

Start with the KPI tiles, then use **Product Net Sales Over Time** to see whether sales moved steadily or spiked.

Use the leaderboards to answer two different questions:

- **Top Products** tells you which product names are winning.
- **Top SKUs** tells you which exact variant or SKU is winning.

The detail table is for auditing. Use it when a product looks wrong or when you need the exact SKU, variant, units, discount, and revenue.

## Common misreads

- **Product Net Sales is not store Total Sales.** Product pages are line-item based and may not include every order-level adjustment.
- **Units and revenue can disagree.** A low-price product can sell many units but contribute less revenue.
- **Discounts matter.** A product can look strong on gross demand but weak after discounting.
- **SKU names can be messy.** If a SKU is blank or inconsistent, use product title and variant title together.

## Drilldown moves

- If sales rose, check whether the increase came from units, price mix, or discounts.
- If a product dropped, compare the SKU leaderboard to see whether one variant is responsible.
- If discounts rose, sort the detail table by discount and inspect the affected products.
- If product demand changed, open [**Attribution**](https://bratrax.com/canvas/campaign_deep_dive) to see which campaign or channel drove it.

## Good Claude questions

- "Which products drove the sales increase last week?"
- "Which SKUs sold the most units but generated low net sales?"
- "Did discounts increase for our top products?"
- "Which products should I inspect based on falling revenue?"
